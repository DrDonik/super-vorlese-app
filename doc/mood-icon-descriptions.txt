Mood icon descriptions for the shared reading memory feature.
Derived from the emotional arc of "Tinka Knitterflügel – Heldin in Ringelsocken".
Feed these to nano banana one at a time. Suggested style: simple hand-drawn sketch,
expressive minimal character, warm ink line on cream/off-white, small square icon format,
no text or labels in the image.

---

1. CRASH-AND-STILL-GRINNING
A small figure sitting in a crumpled heap, hair dishevelled, a smudge on one cheek, grinning despite having clearly just tumbled. Triumphant chaos.

2. DETERMINED CHIN-UP
A face with a firmly set jaw, brow furrowed, chin tilted slightly upward. Stubborn resolve. The expression of someone who will try again even after failing.

3. TUMMY BUTTERFLIES
A small figure pressing both hands flat against their middle, eyes wide and a touch scared, a nervous half-smile. Something important is about to happen.

4. MISCHIEF BREWING
Eyes narrowed to happy crescents, a wide sidelong grin, one finger raised. The face of someone who has just had a very good bad idea.

5. WIDE-EYED WONDER
Mouth open in a perfect O, eyes wide as possible, breath clearly held. Something magical has just appeared and the body forgot to move.

6. RIGHTEOUS STOMP
A tiny figure with fists planted on hips, one foot mid-stamp, brow low. Full of indignation — not cruel anger, but the hot feeling that something is simply not fair.

7. SLUMPED LOW
A figure sitting with knees pulled up, chin resting on them, shoulders curved inward. Quiet, private sadness. Not crying, just very still.

8. FIST IN THE AIR
Standing tall, one arm punched straight up, full-body yes. The moment something finally worked.

9. SNEAKY AND ALERT
Body crouched low, eyes darting sideways, one finger pressed to lips. Holding breath, trying very hard not to exist.

10. COZY PILE
Two or three small shapes huddled warmly together, overlapping, the suggestion of shared warmth and a secret that belongs only to them.

11. GLORIOUSLY DIZZY
A small figure with stars orbiting their head, eyes spiraling in opposite directions, arms out for balance. Confused in the best possible way.

12. QUIET LISTENING
A figure leaning very slightly toward something unseen, expression open and soft. Not waiting — actually hearing.

13. FIZZING EXCITEMENT
A figure shaking with barely-contained energy, slightly blurred at the edges, a grin that is too big to be held in any longer.

14. BRAVE BUT WOBBLY
Eyes steady and a little afraid at the same time, chin up, fists loosely at sides. Scared. Going anyway.

15. PUFFED-CHEEK EXHALE
Cheeks puffed mid-exhale, eyes scrunched shut, shoulders visibly dropping. The body's own sigh of relief after something that had to go right finally did.

16. LIP-OUT SULK
Looking away, lip slightly jutted, brow knotted. Hurt pride that hasn't decided yet whether to forgive.

17. CONTAINED GLOW
A quiet, warm smile — the kind you can't quite stop — eyes a little soft. Something good happened and it's being held carefully.

18. SILLY SERIOUS
Something completely absurd occurring (a creature upside-down, something impossibly stacked) while the face watches with total deadpan calm.

19. WATERY SMILE
A small smile and shining eyes at the same time. Something precious ending or being held in memory.

20. ARMS-WIDE FREE
A figure with arms thrown open, hair streaming, completely unguarded. The feeling of not having to be careful for one moment.

21. LACHKRAMPF
A small figure tipped sideways, both hands clutching the belly, mouth wide open, eyes squeezed into laughing lines. Helpless, full-body laughter that can't be stopped.

22. PEEKING THROUGH FINGERS
A figure with both hands over the face but two fingers spread open to peek through, shoulders hunched up. Too thrilling to watch, too thrilling to look away.

23. CLUTCHED-CLOSE FEELING
A figure hugging an unseen something to the chest with both arms, eyes closed, a soft pang in the expression. A story that landed somewhere tender and is being held there.

24. ONE-MORE-PLEASE
A figure leaning eagerly forward, both hands reaching out, eyebrows lifted high in hopeful request. The whole body asking for it not to be over yet.

25. MELTING SLEEPY
A figure half-collapsed and boneless, eyelids drooping, one cheek already sinking toward a cushion, a faint content smile. Lulled all the way down.

26. HMMM, NOT SURE
A figure with head tilted, one eyebrow up and the mouth pulled to one side, one shoulder shrugged. Honestly unconvinced — it didn't quite land.

27. THAT'S TOO MUCH
A small figure leaning back and away, both palms raised outward, eyes a little overwhelmed. Not scared exactly — just more than was wanted right now.

28. ON-THE-EDGE LEAN
A figure perched forward off the seat, whole body angled toward the unseen page, breath held. Pulled bodily into what happens next.

29. SLOW NOD OF GETTING IT
A figure with a quiet, knowing half-smile and eyes slightly narrowed in recognition, one slow nod implied. The click of something finally making sense.

30. WARM AND FULL
A figure sitting back with hands resting loosely in the lap, a gentle satisfied smile, shoulders fully relaxed. Pleasantly done — the contented full feeling at the end of a good story.

31. SECRETLY MOVED
A figure turning the face slightly away, one hand brushing quickly at the corner of an eye, pretending it was nothing. Touched more than they want to admit.

32. AGAIN-FROM-THE-START
A figure flipping an unseen book back to its beginning with both hands, eager grin, eyebrows raised. Already wanting to live the whole thing over.

33. SCARY SHIVERS
A figure hunched small, collar or blanket-edge pulled up over the nose with both hands, shoulders drawn up and trembling, eyes wide and fixed on one spot. Properly frightened — the good kind, the kind you want a hand to hold through.

34. GLEEFUL YUCK
A figure recoiling with the nose scrunched tight and the tongue half out, one hand flung up between the face and whatever it is. Delighted revulsion — the delicious horror of something truly, gloriously gross.

35. REAL TEARS
A figure with two shining tracks down the cheeks and a trembling mouth, not hiding the face, looking openly up. Unashamedly sad — the story earned every tear and they are being let to fall.

36. LOST THE THREAD
A figure with both palms turned up and empty, head cocked to one side, eyebrows pulled together over a plain blink of puzzlement. Not playing along — genuinely lost, and honest enough to show it.

37. POLITELY ELSEWHERE
A figure slouched sideways, one cheek propped on a fist, eyes wandered off the unseen page, the very start of a stifled yawn. Drifted away — it simply didn't take hold this time.

38. JAW-DROP TWIST
A figure rocked back, both hands flown up to the sides of the face, mouth a wide-open gasp, eyes enormous. Not frightened — astonished. It turned out nothing like what was expected.

39. KISS ACROSS THE MILES
A figure pressing fingertips to the lips and sweeping the hand outward toward someone unseen, eyes warm and a little soft, the other hand flat over the heart. The feeling aimed not at the story but at the person on the far end of it.

40. PROUD OF YOU
A figure mid-clap, hands just meeting, head tilted with a beaming wide smile turned toward someone off to one side. Not pride in oneself — a warm bursting pride held up for somebody else.

The entries below were added to thicken under-served clusters for the balanced board draw (issue #77). A board guarantees a minimum from each emotional region, so these previously single-image feelings — scared, overwhelmed, anger, sad, courage, and the two partner-directed gestures — give those guarantees a fitting, varied word rather than one repeated tile. Same style as above; numbering continues so existing ids stay stable.

41. PEERING OUT FROM HIDING
A small figure ducked low behind the edge of a blanket or the back of a chair, only the eyes and the grip of two hands showing over the top, brows tented with worry. Frightened and taking cover — watching the scary part from just barely far enough away.

42. HANDS OVER THE EARS
A figure curling inward with both hands clamped over the ears, eyes squeezed shut, shoulders hunched up tight. Not frightened exactly — just too much, too loud, too fast, and needing it all to stop for a second.

43. THE HOT WHOLE-BODY NO
A figure stiff with fury, both fists clenched down hard at the sides, shoulders jumped up, brows slammed low over a tight-pressed mouth. Not a stamp and not a sulk — the hot, full-body NO when something is simply, deeply not fair.

44. HOLDING BACK THE TEARS
A figure with chin wobbling and eyes brimming but not spilling, lips pressed and pulled in, blinking hard and looking up to keep it in. Sad and trying so hard to be brave about it — the tears right there at the edge.

45. ONE BIG BRAVE BREATH
A figure squaring the shoulders and drawing one big breath in, chest lifted, eyes closed for the half-second before, hands loosely at the sides. Gathering itself right at the edge of doing the brave thing.

46. HAND ACROSS THE DISTANCE
A figure reaching one open hand flat toward someone unseen, as if pressing it to a pane of glass, eyes warm and the other hand resting over the heart. Wanting to touch the person on the far end when the miles won't allow it.

47. THUMBS UP FOR YOU
A figure with both thumbs thrown up and a beaming grin turned off to one side, leaning toward someone unseen. Not „I did it" — „you did it", cheered straight across to somebody else.
