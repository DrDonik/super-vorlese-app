Mood icon descriptions for the shared reading memory feature.
Derived from the emotional arc of "Tinka Knitterflügel – Heldin in Ringelsocken".
Feed these to nano banana one at a time. Suggested style: simple hand-drawn sketch,
expressive minimal character, warm ink line on cream/off-white, small square icon format,
no text or labels in the image.

---

1. CRASH-AND-STILL-GRINNING
A small figure sitting in a crumpled heap, hair dishevelled, a smudge on one cheek,
grinning despite having clearly just tumbled. Triumphant chaos.

2. DETERMINED CHIN-UP
A face with a firmly set jaw, brow furrowed, chin tilted slightly upward. Stubborn
resolve. The expression of someone who will try again even after failing.

3. TUMMY BUTTERFLIES
A small figure pressing both hands flat against their middle, eyes wide and a touch
scared, a nervous half-smile. Something important is about to happen.

4. MISCHIEF BREWING
Eyes narrowed to happy crescents, a wide sidelong grin, one finger raised. The face
of someone who has just had a very good bad idea.

5. WIDE-EYED WONDER
Mouth open in a perfect O, eyes wide as possible, breath clearly held. Something
magical has just appeared and the body forgot to move.

6. RIGHTEOUS STOMP
A tiny figure with fists planted on hips, one foot mid-stamp, brow low. Full of
indignation — not cruel anger, but the hot feeling that something is simply not fair.

7. SLUMPED LOW
A figure sitting with knees pulled up, chin resting on them, shoulders curved inward.
Quiet, private sadness. Not crying, just very still.

8. FIST IN THE AIR
Standing tall, one arm punched straight up, full-body yes. The moment something
finally worked.

9. SNEAKY AND ALERT
Body crouched low, eyes darting sideways, one finger pressed to lips. Holding breath,
trying very hard not to exist.

10. COZY PILE
Two or three small shapes huddled warmly together, overlapping, the suggestion of
shared warmth and a secret that belongs only to them.

11. GLORIOUSLY DIZZY
A small figure with stars orbiting their head, eyes spiraling in opposite directions,
arms out for balance. Confused in the best possible way.

12. QUIET LISTENING
A figure leaning very slightly toward something unseen, expression open and soft.
Not waiting — actually hearing.

13. FIZZING EXCITEMENT
A figure shaking with barely-contained energy, slightly blurred at the edges, a grin
that is too big to be held in any longer.

14. BRAVE BUT WOBBLY
Eyes steady and a little afraid at the same time, chin up, fists loosely at sides.
Scared. Going anyway.

15. PUFFED-CHEEK EXHALE
Cheeks puffed mid-exhale, eyes scrunched shut, shoulders visibly dropping. The body's
own sigh of relief after something that had to go right finally did.

16. LIP-OUT SULK
Looking away, lip slightly jutted, brow knotted. Hurt pride that hasn't decided yet
whether to forgive.

17. CONTAINED GLOW
A quiet, warm smile — the kind you can't quite stop — eyes a little soft. Something
good happened and it's being held carefully.

18. SILLY SERIOUS
Something completely absurd occurring (a creature upside-down, something impossibly
stacked) while the face watches with total deadpan calm.

19. WATERY SMILE
A small smile and shining eyes at the same time. Something precious ending or being
held in memory.

20. ARMS-WIDE FREE
A figure with arms thrown open, hair streaming, completely unguarded. The feeling of
not having to be careful for one moment.
